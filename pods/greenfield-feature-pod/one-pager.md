# Greenfield Feature Pod

The Greenfield Feature Pod builds new features against the product owner's intent baseline, with design approval before any code is written.

## What you get
See the pod card: the client outcomes come from `pod.yaml` (`client_gets`).

## How it works
Truminds engineers drive AI agents for each role in the pod. The agents propose; code checks every
artifact; your people answer questions, approve gates and merge. Nothing is merged or deployed without a person.

## Track record
The portal adds measured results here from telemetry when enough runs exist. No figures are written by hand.
