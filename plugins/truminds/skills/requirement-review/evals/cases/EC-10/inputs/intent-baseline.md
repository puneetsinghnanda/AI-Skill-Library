# Intent baseline {#intent-baseline}

## Goals {#goals}
Members can borrow and return items without queuing at the desk.

## Constraints {#constraints}
A loan lasts 14 days.

## Renewals {#renewals}
A loan can be renewed once, for another 14 days, if nobody has reserved the item.
