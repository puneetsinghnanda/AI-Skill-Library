# Glossary {#glossary}

## Member {#member}
A person registered with the library.

## Loan {#loan}
One item lent to one member, with a due date.

## Renewal {#renewal}
Extending the due date of a loan.

## Reservation {#reservation}
A member's request to borrow an item that is out on loan.
