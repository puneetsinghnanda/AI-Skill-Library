Personas

Who sees what

One intranet portal, company SSO, two views. The top-bar switch shows each view; in production the view follows the person's role.

BU

Business unit

Raj, Ram, sales and marketing

Goal: understand and sell AI-augmented delivery with credible, client-safe proof.

Top tasks: explain the library, find skills by role, propose a pod, show impact numbers and the capability map, request a pilot.

Lands on: Home (Business) with impact, building blocks, roles and pods.

Never sees: run ids, tickets, repos, engineer names, client names.

EN

Engineers

Software engineers, tech leads, architects, program owners

Goal: deliver client work faster with skills they can trust, and see where runs are stuck.

Top tasks: install the plugin from GitHub, pick a skill or pod workflow, track runs and waiting decisions, check skill quality and system health.

Lands on: Home (Engineer) with my runs, waiting on me and skills for my role.

Sees within their projects only: runs, tickets, records. Names by pseudonym unless they lead the team.

How pods fit each persona

Business unit: sell

Picks the pod for the engagement, shares its one-pager and track record, requests a pilot.

Engineers and tech leads: run

Run the pod's workflows in Claude Code, answer questions, approve gates, review PRs.

Program owners and architects: steer

Watch the pod's runs, waiting decisions and quality; adjust which pod fits which client system.
